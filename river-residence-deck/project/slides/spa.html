The region · thermal and mineral springs

Spas and thermal pools within an hour

Vyšné Ružbachy · 8 km

Historic spa village with thermal mineral springs, an outdoor thermal pool and the travertine lake known as the Crater.

Vrbov thermal park · 35 km

Geothermal water pools open all year, with a view of the High Tatras from the water.

Bardejov spa · 55 km

Mineral-water spa beside Bardejov, a UNESCO World Heritage town with a preserved medieval square.
