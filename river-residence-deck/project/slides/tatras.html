The region · 45 km from the site

The High Tatras on the horizon

The High Tatras are the highest mountains in Slovakia and in the whole Carpathian arc, rising to 2,655 m at Gerlachovský štít. They are a national park and a year-round resort region under an hour's drive from River Residence.

  • Skiing in Tatranská Lomnica and Štrbské Pleso in winter
  • Hiking, mountain lakes and cable cars in summer
  • Hotels, spas and the Poprad–Tatry airport at their foot
  • Visible from the 5th to the 8th floor of the Tatras-facing apartments
