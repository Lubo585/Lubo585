# Južná Európa, UK/Írsko a pan-európsky second-hand: Wallapop, Subito, Vinted, Gumtree, Shpock, Depop

Scope note: profiles reflect the state as of 2025–Oct 2026. Every number is dated and sourced inline. Where official help-centre pages were blocked (Wallapop ayuda, Depop help, Vinted UK help partially), third-party guides are used and flagged as such. Web-search budget ran out before app-store ratings for Subito/Vinted/Shpock/Depop and before Subito/Gumtree AI features could be confirmed; see Gaps.

## Key question 0: Portal profiles — owner, history, scale, revenue

### Takeaway
All six portals changed hands or ownership structure between 2021 and 2026 (Wallapop → Naver 100% Jan 2026; Subito inside the Permira/Blackstone-led Adevinta take-private; Gumtree UK → Ocean Link/58.com Sept 2024; Shpock → Russmedia 2021; Depop Etsy → eBay July 2026; Vinted secondary at €8bn Apr 2026). Vinted (€1.1bn revenue, €10.8bn GMV in 2025) is an order of magnitude bigger than any of the national generalists (Wallapop €116M, Subito ~€92M, Gumtree UK ~£23.5M est.).

## Key question 1: What do these portals do better than a plain free classifieds board like Bazoš?

### Takeaway
All six moved beyond "phone-number classifieds" with in-app chat, in-app payments held in escrow, integrated label-based shipping to lockers/parcel shops, buyer protection with refunds, seller ID/bank verification and ratings; Gumtree is the closest to Bazoš (still free, no native payments) and is also the one losing scale.

### Cited Findings
- Wallapop: in-app encrypted payments, funds released to seller only after buyer confirms item condition, refund if item never arrives/damaged/not as described; chat for local meetups; Wallapop Envíos via Correos office or home; App Store 4.8/5 from 1.6M ratings — [App Store ES](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/wallapop-vende-y-compra/id692753615)
- Subito TuttoSubito: buyer pays on platform, seller paid after delivery; delivery to >25,000 pick-up points (shops, lockers, post offices) or home; Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay; refund if package not received or item not as described — [Subito](https://info.subito.it/tuttosubito.htm)
- Vinted: Buyer Protection/"Vinted fee" funds refunds if item lost, damaged or significantly not as described (non-pro sellers); paid Item Verification (£10) and Electronics Verification (£5) add-ons — [Vinted UK pricelist](https://www.vinted.co.uk/pricelist); [Vinted UK help 342](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/342-buyer-protection-fee-on-vinted)
- Vinted was rated highest by both buyers (75%) and sellers (78%) and had the lowest scam rate (22% buyers / 11% sellers) in Which?'s Jan 2024 survey of 1,318 buyers and 1,402 sellers; Gumtree 29%/16%; Shpock 53%/26%; Depop 57%/23%; Which? attributes high complaint rates on Nextdoor to lack of standard buyer protection — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/policy-and-insight/article/a-third-of-buyers-have-experienced-a-scam-on-popular-second-hand-marketplaces-in-the-last-two-years-which-warns-aEea68N4nnah)
- Depop: payments stay in-app (Apple Pay, Google Pay, cards, Klarna) which lets Depop mediate disputes and refunds; sellers must add a bank account and verify identity; buyers in AU/UK/US get full refund within 30 days if item missing, damaged or significantly different — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depop)
- Shpock: Buy Now button, in-app wallet, Parcel2Go delivery, Buyer Protection up to £1,000 (2019) — [ChannelX](https://channelx.world/2019/11/shpock-classified-ads-evolves-into-new-sphock-marketplace/)
- Gumtree: still "sell for free" except pets and business accounts; native product is chat with read indicators; no payments/protection advertised — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id487946174); business products: 60-day ad life, up to 9 photos, free edits, Featured, Homepage Spotlight (7 days), Urgent tag, Pro Console with account manager — [Gumtree for Business](https://www.gumtree.com/info/life/gumtree-for-business/services/)
- Wallapop shipping partner history: Correos integrated into app (2019) and Seur added for home pickup — [Alimarket Correos](https://www.alimarket.es/logistica/noticia/249715/correos-gestionara-los-envios-de-wallapop); [Alimarket Seur](https://www.alimarket.es/logistica/noticia/323205/wallapop-se-apoya-en-seur-para-mejorar-los-envios-de-sus-usuarios); Mangopay provided the wallet/escrow infrastructure that monetised Wallapop — [Mangopay](https://blog.mangopay.com/en/home/how-mangopay-helped-monetize-wallapops-classified-ads-platform)

### Inferences
- The differentiators versus Bazoš cluster into: (1) escrow + refund guarantee, (2) label-based shipping to dense PUDO/locker networks, (3) in-app chat replacing phone exposure, (4) verified seller identity + ratings. Portals that only added chat (Gumtree) show stagnating traffic and are being sold at low valuations; portals that added escrow (Wallapop, Subito, Vinted) report rising transaction revenue.
- For Tržko, the Slovak/Czech analogue of the Correos/Poste Italiane/Royal Mail integrations would be Packeta (Zásielkovňa) and Slovenská pošta/Česká pošta boxes, plus Alza/GLS lockers.

### Gaps
- No source quantifies what share of Wallapop/Subito transactions go through escrow vs. cash meetups.

## Key question 2: Exact mechanics and pricing of buyer protection / escrow and shipping

### Takeaway
All transactional portals charge the buyer, not the seller: Subito €1.20 + 5% (≤€300) / 4.5% (>€300) capped at €91.20; Wallapop €1.69 flat up to €13, then €0.69 + 7.5% up to €657, then €50 flat to €2,500 (third-party table, May 2025); Vinted "usually 3–8% + £0.30–£0.80"; Depop charges sellers only processing (2.9% + £0.30 UK) and has shifted to buyer-side fees; Shpock relies on PayPal Buyer Protection and now charges sellers a subscription.

### Cited Findings

**Wallapop (Protección Wallapop / Wallapop Envíos)**
- Buyer pays item + insurance + shipping ("El comprador paga el artículo, el seguro y los gastos de envío"); Protección fee: €1–13 purchase = €1.69 fixed; €13.01–657 = €0.69 + 7.5% of item value; €657.01–2,500 = €50 fixed (guide dated 1 May 2025, warns Wallapop updates prices periodically) — [AndroidGuías](https://androidguias.com/envios-wallapop/)
- Shipping price table (same source): Correos office pickup from €2.99 (0–2 kg) to €15.49 (20–30 kg); home delivery €3.49–16.49; GLS point €2.39–14.49; Balearic routes €5.50–42.95; bicycles €12.75–35.65; Seur home pickup +€2.25 paid by seller; "Method" bulky-item service charges seller 10% of sale price; no service to Ceuta, Melilla, Canarias — [AndroidGuías](https://androidguias.com/envios-wallapop/)
- A 2026 guide states Wallapop charges the buyer ~10% commission when Wallapop Envíos is used and nothing on in-person sales — [SellyGenie](https://sellygenie.com/es/blog/milanuncios-vs-wallapop) (consistent with 0.69 + 7.5% plus shipping ≈ 10% effective)
- Official position: management costs, protection insurance and shipping are always charged to the buyer; seller pays nothing — [MundoBytes](https://mundobytes.com/wallapop-quien-se-hace-cargo-de-los-gastos-de-envio-tasas-de-transaccion/)
- Escrow release: funds go to seller only after buyer confirms the product's condition — [App Store ES](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/wallapop-vende-y-compra/id692753615)
- Carriers: Correos, Seur, GLS, Method; a 2025 OCU complaint mentions InPost as insured-shipping partner — [AndroidGuías](https://androidguias.com/envios-wallapop/); [OCU complaint](https://www.ocu.org/reclamar/lista-reclamaciones-publicas/protecci-C3-B3n-envios-wallapop/30d8311a4b6449a51e)

**Subito (TuttoSubito)**
- Buyer Protection fee: €1.20 fixed + 5% of item price up to €300, or 4.5% above €300; total capped at €91.20; seller pays no commission ("vendere non costa nulla"); shipping always paid by buyer, price depends on package size and destination, shown in order summary; parcels up to 20 kg; carriers Poste Italiane (cannot be deselected), InPost (home, InPost Point or Locker), BRT, UPS; >25,000 pickup points — [Subito](https://info.subito.it/tuttosubito.htm); [LetteraSenzaBusta](https://www.letterasenzabusta.com/news/1005/come-spedire-su-subito-quando-vendi.htm)
- Payout: private sellers see funds 3–6 working days after buyer confirms receipt; if buyer does not confirm, auto-release 3 days after delivery notification (TuttoSubito shipping) or 20 days after payment (self-shipped); business sellers ("Rivenditore"): 14-day withdrawal right, release at 17 days after delivery notification / 24 days after shipment; buyer has 7 days to collect from pickup point — [Subito](https://info.subito.it/tuttosubito.htm)
- Seller must create label within 48 h and ship within 6 days — [Assistenza Subito](https://assistenza.subito.it/hc/it/articles/5233241977117-Spedire-l-oggetto)

**Vinted**
- Fee "usually 3% to 8% + £0.3 to £0.8", computed from item characteristics, order value and single-item vs bundle; VAT included; funds refunds for lost/damaged/SNAD items from non-professional sellers — [Vinted UK help 342](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/342-buyer-protection-fee-on-vinted); [Vinted UK pricelist](https://www.vinted.co.uk/pricelist)
- US fee quoted by third parties as 5% + $0.70 — [Flipsail](https://www.flipsail.io/blog/vinted-usa); older UK rule "3% for orders under £500" — third-party, likely outdated — [Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/fees/vinted-seller-fees)
- Sellers pay no listing or success fee; buyer side monetised via protection fee plus margin on shipping and payments — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/vinted)
- Oct 2026 terms: fee renamed "Vinted fee" (same calculation), refund rights moved to a separate Refund Policy covering lost, damaged, SNAD and counterfeit items; Vinted Pro listed in 11 European markets — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/vinted-us-uk-australia-terms-october-2026/)
- Which? flags Vinted's two-day window for raising issues; only 46% of complaining sellers and 62% of buyers were happy with resolution — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/policy-and-insight/article/a-third-of-buyers-have-experienced-a-scam-on-popular-second-hand-marketplaces-in-the-last-two-years-which-warns-aEea68N4nnah)
- UK shipping network (Aug 2025): buyers can collect at Royal Mail's 2,000 lockers and ~8,000 Collect+ Parcelshops; plus 11,500 Post Office branches and 1,200 Customer Service Points — >22,000 locations; Royal Mail home delivery remains — [IPC](https://www.ipc.be/news-portal/general-news/2025/08/08/07/26/vinted-members-can-now-collect-and-send-orders); [ChannelX](https://channelx.world/2025/08/vinted-buyers-can-now-collect-at-royal-mail-lockers-collect-parcelshops/)
- Vinted Go: >10,000 lockers/PUDO points in France (Oct 2025), first lockers in Belgium Oct 2025; operates in BE, FR, NL, PT, ES; new sorting centre in France; external-client testing begun — [RetailDetail](https://www.retaildetail.eu/news/general/vinted-go-opens-its-first-lockers-in-belgium/); [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/business/vinted-reaches-1-1-billion-euros-in-revenue-for-2025-accepting-a-strategic-drop-in-profits/2026040987347)

**Depop**
- UK sellers: no selling fee; payment processing 2.9% + £0.30 per sale (Depop Payments/Stripe); US 3.3% + $0.45; sellers outside US/UK/AU still pay 10% selling fee + PayPal processing — [Vendoo UK](https://uk.blog.vendoo.co/guide-to-depop-fees-for-uk-sellers-buyers); [Nifty](https://nifty.ai/post/selling-on-depop-vs-etsy); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depop) (US fee removal 18 July 2024)
- Boosted Listings: 8% of item price + shipping charged only when a boosted item sells; UK rate raised to 12% from 3 Dec 2025; US/AU still 8% — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/depop-uk-ups-boosted-listing-advertising-fee/)
- Depop "moved from seller fees to buyer fees in the UK, then expanded that change to the US in 2024" — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/depop-uk-ups-boosted-listing-advertising-fee/) (exact buyer fee amount not stated; see Gaps)

**Shpock**
- 2019 model: free to buy and sell, Buyer Protection to £1,000, 3-day claim window, Parcel2Go shipping — [ChannelX](https://channelx.world/2019/11/shpock-classified-ads-evolves-into-new-sphock-marketplace/)
- 2025–26 model: listing now requires a paid membership (reviewers cite ~£1 rising to ~£5/month), 29p to reactivate an expired listing; Shpock's replies describe Standard membership (unlimited listings, no ads, 90-day listing life, two free promotions/month, support within 3 h) and Premium (unlimited reactivations, "Reactivate All"); Shpock says buyers are covered by PayPal Buyer Protection — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/shpock.com)

**Gumtree**

## Key question 3: How does Vinted's "free for sellers, buyer pays protection fee" model work financially, and has it been copied?

### Takeaway
Vinted's buyer-paid fee plus shipping/payment margin produced €1.1bn revenue on €10.8bn GMV (≈10% take) with €151M adjusted EBITDA and €137M FCF in 2025; the model has been copied by Subito (TuttoSubito), Wallapop (Protección), Depop (UK/US 2024), eBay UK (Feb 2025 private-seller buyer fee) and Kleinanzeigen (Käuferschutz).

### Cited Findings
- Vinted take: revenue €1.1bn on GMV €10.8bn (FY2025) → ~10.2% of GMV; adjusted EBITDA €151M (13.7% margin), FCF €137M; profit deliberately reduced to fund Germany, categories, Vinted Go and payments — [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/business/vinted-reaches-1-1-billion-euros-in-revenue-for-2025-accepting-a-strategic-drop-in-profits/2026040987347)
- Revenue sources: buyer protection fee (main), shipping margin, payments margin; sellers pay nothing; Vinted Pay is a wholly-owned EMI (2023) and Vinted Go an owned/partner locker network — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/vinted)
- Vinted Pay obtained a UK FCA EMI licence (March 2026); European balance users migrated from Mangopay to Vinted Pay from 5 Oct 2026 in ten countries; US/AU stay on Adyen — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/vinted-us-uk-australia-terms-october-2026/)
- Profit history: €17.8M (2023) → €76.7M (2024) → €62M (2025) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinted)
- Copies: Subito TuttoSubito "vendere non costa nulla", buyer pays €1.20 + 5%/4.5% — [Subito](https://info.subito.it/tuttosubito.htm); Wallapop buyer pays protection + shipping, seller free — [AndroidGuías](https://androidguias.com/envios-wallapop/); Depop dropped seller commissions in UK/US (2024) and moved to buyer fees — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/depop-uk-ups-boosted-listing-advertising-fee/); eBay UK introduced a buyer-facing fee for private sales from 4 Feb 2025 and later reduced it — [ChannelX Oct 2024](https://channelx.world/2024/10/new-ebay-buyer-facing-fee-coming-to-uk-in-2025/); [ChannelX Jul 2025](https://channelx.world/2025/07/ebay-uk-reduce-buyer-protection-fees/); eBay also ended private-seller fees in France and Italy, moving the cost to buyers — [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/ebay-ends-private-seller-fees-on-its-french-and-italian-sites-and-moves-the-cost-to-buyers-through-a-new-buyer-protection-fee/); Kleinanzeigen (DE) sells a "Käuferschutz" buyer-protection add-on — [Kleinanzeigen guide](https://digitalocean.wpcompress.com/blog/kleinanzeigen-kaeuferschutz-price-fees-and)
- Which? rated Depop two stars for fee reasonableness before Depop changed its fee structure — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/policy-and-insight/article/a-third-of-buyers-have-experienced-a-scam-on-popular-second-hand-marketplaces-in-the-last-two-years-which-warns-aEea68N4nnah)

### Inferences
- The model works because supply (listings) is the scarce side in C2C; zero seller fees maximise inventory, while buyers tolerate a 5–10% fee in exchange for refunds. Wallapop's 2025 numbers (Envíos €85M vs. subscriptions €27M) show a generalist can replicate it, but Wallapop still runs at a loss, so the fee alone does not guarantee profitability without Vinted-scale volume.
- Owning payments (Vinted Pay) and logistics (Vinted Go) captures the margin that Wallapop/Subito pay to Mangopay/Correos/Poste; this is a scale play not available to a Slovak startup, which should instead negotiate margin-sharing with Packeta/Slovenská pošta and a PSP.

### Gaps
- No public split of Vinted revenue into fee vs shipping vs payments.
- Depop's current buyer-fee formula not retrieved.

## Key question 5: Documented weaknesses and user complaints

### Takeaway
Complaints cluster around (a) buyer-protection claims rejected or slow (Vinted, Wallapop), (b) scams on platforms without escrow (Gumtree, Shpock, Depop per Which?), (c) automated bans/counterfeit flags (Vinted), and (d) monetisation backlash (Shpock subscriptions, Depop boost fee hikes).

### Cited Findings
- Which?
