## Key Question 0: Who owns these platforms, how big are they, and what exactly do they charge? (platform profiles)

### Takeaway
Three of the seven are now profitable at scale on very different models: Mercari (JPY 1.2T GMV, 24M MAU, 10% commission + fintech), Karrot/당근 (21M+ MAU, ~96% of revenue from hyperlocal ads, escrow only since 2024–2026), and Dubizzle (46% adjusted EBITDA margin from dealer/agency subscriptions). Xianyu/闲鱼 (200M+ MAU) only started charging sellers a 0.6% fee in Sept 2024; Carousell (USD 141M revenue, EBITDA-positive FY2025) and Jiji (free listings, paid promotion) remain classifieds-plus-promotion businesses.

## Key Question 1: What makes Karrot's hyperlocal neighbourhood model so sticky, and what could a small-country portal copy?

### Takeaway
Karrot's stickiness comes from forcing every user through GPS-verified neighbourhood membership (max 2 locations, 2–6 km radius, periodic re-verification), a single visible reputation number (manner temperature starting at 36.5 °C), and then layering non-commerce reasons to open the app daily (community board, part-time jobs, local business profiles, groups) that are monetised through geo-targeted ads. The mechanics are not Korea-specific; the density and small-shop economy of Slovak/Czech towns suit them.

### Cited Findings
- Neighbourhood verification: users must verify identity and location, confirmed via phone signal and GPS; listings shown within a set distance; both parties use nicknames in chat; manner temperature starts at 36.5 °C and is adjusted by transaction reviews; "Nego X" means price not negotiable; sign-up requires a Korean mobile number — [Korea Herald, Mar 2024](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/3348887)
- Users re-verify location by GPS roughly every 30 days; up to two verified locations (e.g. home and work), each requiring physical presence; visibility within one's dong and adjacent areas, radius 2–6 km; manner temperature tiers: 36.5–40 °C standard, 40–50 °C reliable, 50 °C+ highly trusted, <36 °C caution; free giveaways ("Nanum") typically claimed within a minute; chat-to-deal averages <48 h; search alerts via bell icon; convenience-store half-price shipping (GS25/CU) KRW 1,800–2,600 for items ≤5 kg (unofficial guide, Jan/Feb 2026) — [KoreaExperience guide, 2026](https://koreaexperience.com/blog/how-to-use-karrot-danggeun-korea-favorite-second-hand-app-2026)
- Model (2022): connects buyers and sellers within a 6 km radius; sellers post in under 30 seconds; buyers chat privately and meet locally, no shipping; users compare it to an entertaining social app and describe themselves as "addicted"; trust reinforced by location/mobile verification and reputation score; professional sellers excluded to keep community feel; hyperlocal ads the only revenue; O2O partnerships (cleaning, laundry) widen offering; 18M MAU, 30M cumulative users (Aug 2022); author doubts transferability to low-density US suburbs — [HBS Digital Initiative, Nov 2022](https://d3.harvard.edu/platform-digit/submission/a-closer-look-at-south-koreas-beloved-neighborhood-market-platform/index.html)
- Non-commerce surfaces: neighbourhood requests (e.g. bug removal for KRW 10,000), dinner/movie companions, furniture-moving help, shop owners seeking part-timers — [Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/3348887)
- 2025 engagement: 190M transaction connections; >50M part-time-job applications; groups (모임) membership +125% YoY; 2.65M business profiles; ads use regional targeting by living area and interests, increasingly tied to jobs, real estate and used cars — [VentureSquare](https://www.venturesquare.net/1069052/)
- Cumulative "regulars" (단골) connected to local businesses via BizProfile surpassed 10 million (June 2026) — [Herald Corp](https://biz.heraldcorp.com/article/10776174)
- Karrot deliberately did not offer escrow early because most trades were face-to-face, hence lower fraud exposure than parcel-heavy rivals Bunjang/Junggonara — [Byline Network, June 2026](https://byline.network/2026/06/17_291828734/)
- Karrot ships in >560 regions across Canada, US, UK, Japan (company-linked wiki, undated) — [EverybodyWiki: Karrot](https://en.everybodywiki.com/Karrot); >1M registered Canadian users after 2 years — [BetaKit, May 2024](https://betakit.com/rt-the-retail-times-south-korean-retail-unicorn-to-set-roots-in-toronto/)
- User criticism (App Store KR, 3.9/5): out-of-area users can only chat with a seller after "buying" the listing; AI-based auto-suspensions for suspected dealer activity with templated support replies; immediate deletion of accounts flagged under 14 — [App Store KR](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id1018769995)
- Users questioned whether adding parcel/escrow conflicts with Karrot's local direct-trade identity; Karrot answered that the trade board stays free and escrow is optional — [Bizhankook](http://bizhankook.com/articles/28238.html)
- Korea-wide context: police data put 2025 direct-trade (used-goods) fraud losses at KRW 874.07B, 3.4x 2021; common scam = fake escrow pop-up asking the buyer to re-send money for a "missing fee" — [Maeil Shinmun, Oct 2026](https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026101600140401394)

### Inferences
- The copyable core for Slovakia/Czechia: (1) GPS-verified "my town" membership with 1–2 locations and a radius slider; (2) a single, prominently displayed trust score that starts neutral and moves with reviews (the thermometer metaphor is cheap to build and widely understood); (3) a local community/jobs/services tab that gives daily reasons to open the app; (4) local business profiles + geo-targeted self-serve ads as the revenue engine; (5) distance-based "meet up vs. parcel" choice in the listing form. Bratislava (~475k), Košice, Prague (1.3M) and Brno have Korea-like apartment density.
- What not to copy blindly: Korea-only phone-number gating, and the out-of-area chat block that users dislike; for a two-country portal, allow cross-region chat but flag it.
- Karrot's weak App Store rating (3.9) despite dominance shows that aggressive automated moderation is the main UX cost of the trust model; appeals processes matter.

### Gaps
- No primary Karrot document was fetched describing the exact manner-temperature algorithm (weights of reviews, response speed, no-shows); only the 36.5 °C start and review-driven adjustment are confirmed by Korea Herald.
- The 30-day re-verification cadence and 2–6 km radius come from an unofficial expat guide, not Karrot's help centre.

## Key Question 2: How do Mercari and Xianyu make escrow + anonymous shipping frictionless, and what do they charge?

### Takeaway
Mercari bundles escrow into every sale (platform holds payment until the buyer rates), charges a flat 10% to the seller, and sells anonymous shipping as a menu of fixed-price options (JPY 160–750+) bought inside the app and dropped at any 7-Eleven/FamilyMart/Lawson/post office with a QR code; Xianyu rides on Alipay's 担保交易 (guaranteed payment) with a 0.6% seller fee capped at 60 yuan. Karrot copied the Mercari pattern in 2025–26 with CJ Logistics and a 3.3% buyer-paid fee.

### Cited Findings
- Mercari Japan shipping menu (prices as of Nov 2025, tax incl.): らくらくメルカリ便 (Yamato) — Nekoposu 210 yen (≤3 cm thick, ≤1 kg), Takkyubin Compact 450 yen + 70 yen box, Takkyubin 750 yen+ (sizes 60–200, 2–30 kg); ゆうゆうメルカリ便 (Japan Post) — Yu-Packet Post mini 160 yen + 20 yen envelope, Yu-Packet Post 215 yen, Yu-Packet 230 yen, Yu-Packet Plus 455 yen + 65 yen box, Yu-Pack 750 yen+ (sizes 60–170); エコメルカリ便 flat 730 yen up to size 100 (limited areas); 梱包・発送たのメル便 from 1,700 yen incl. packing for furniture (sizes 60–450). Drop-off: Yamato counters, FamilyMart, 7-Eleven, home pickup; Japan Post counters, post boxes, Lawson; Eco via SMARI boxes. Anonymity: waybill carries encrypted destination; carrier applies the address label; must be selected at listing time (cannot be switched to anonymous after purchase); not available for returns — [Mercari column, Nov 2025](https://jp-news.mercari.com/contents/1029)
- Flow: after a sale the app generates a QR/2D code; seller scans it at the store terminal (FamilyMart, 7-Eleven, Lawson) and hands over the parcel; Mercari compensates full item price if a Mercari-bin parcel is lost or damaged — [Appllio shipping guide, 2021](https://appllio.com/mercari-best-shipping-guide) (older prices in that guide: Nekoposu 175, Compact 380, Takkyubin 700–1,600 yen — shows ~20% rises since 2021)

- Trust add-ons (2025–26): Mercari Appraisal Center established Sept 2025; "Safe Appraisal" (あんしん鑑定) auto-enabled from March 2026; "Full coverage support program" from 1 July 2025 — [Mercari Fact Book](https://www.speakerdeck.com/mercari_inc/mercari-fact-book-en)

### Inferences
- The frictionless ingredients common to Mercari/Karrot: (a) shipping is chosen at listing time, not negotiated in chat; (b) one QR code replaces address entry and labels; (c) pricing is flat/national and shown in-app; (d) anonymity is a by-product of the carrier printing the address, not a separate feature; (e) escrow is default, with the fee either folded into a seller commission (Mercari 10%) or shown as a small buyer fee (Karrot 3.3%, Carousell ~2.5%, Xianyu 0.6% seller).
- For Slovakia/Czechia, the local analogue is Packeta/Zásilkovna (Z-Box) and Alza-box networks plus Slovenská pošta/Česká pošta Balíkovna: a Mercari-style "anonymous box-to-box" flat tariff could be the single biggest trust upgrade over Bazoš, which has no escrow.
- Mercari's 2024 return-fraud episode shows escrow shifts fraud toward the return leg; a "Full coverage" guarantee and appraisal centre were the answers.

### Gaps
- Exact Xianyu escrow mechanics (auto-release timing after delivery, dispute window, 验货宝 inspection fee) could not be verified — the search budget ran out before fetching Xianyu's rules pages.
- Official Mercari help-centre price page returned 404; prices are from Mercari's own editorial column (Nov 2025) which carries a "may differ now" disclaimer.

## Key Question 3: Which AI features have shipped (photo-to-listing, pricing, chat assistants) and how are they received?

### Takeaway
All three Asian leaders now ship photo-to-listing AI: Mercari "AI出品サポート" (Sept 2024, GPT-4o mini, 3 taps), Carousell "List with AI" (Gemini, video or photos, median listing <2 min, +20% listings), and Xianyu "AI相机" (March 2026, 5 seconds, Qwen-trained pricing, 12M users/50M items). Reception is positive on speed but Xianyu's June 2026 incident (AI auto-listing private photos, including museum artefacts and a pet cat) shows the consent/accuracy risk.

### Cited Findings
- Mercari "AI出品サポート" launched 10 Sept 2024: upload photos + pick category → AI fills title, description, condition and price; listing completes in as few as 3 taps; on by default; rolled out in stages; suggestions can be inaccurate and are editable; motivated by a 2023 survey where users abandoned listings because they did not know how much description to write — [ECnomikata, Sept 2024](https://ecnomikata.com/ecnews/ecmall/44437/); [BCN Retail](https://www.bcnretail.com/topics_detail8/20240911_453500.html); plan to let AI also decide category — [Impress Watch](https://www.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1622732.html)
- Powered by GPT-4o mini; suggests categories then generates titles/descriptions — [OpenAI customer story](https://openai.com/index/mercari); launched to >20M users within 2 months — [Google Cloud: Mercari](https://cloud.google.com/customers/mercari-data)

- AI pricing needs a local comp database; Mercari uses its own sold-item history ("Price Search"), Xianyu a Qwen model on its GMV. Tržko could seed pricing from Bazoš/Tržko historical listings.

### Gaps

## Key Question 4: How do these apps keep listing creation under one minute?

### Takeaway
Under-a-minute listing is achieved by removing text entry: photo-first flows (Karrot <30 s, Xianyu ~30 s with voice and barcode, Mercari barcode "≤1 min" and AI "3 taps"), defaults chosen at listing time (shipping method, price negotiability), and AI auto-fill that the seller only confirms.

### Cited Findings
- Karrot: sellers can post in under 30 seconds; chat replaces negotiation forms; "Nanum" (free), "Nego X/O" tags encode negotiability — [HBS Digital Initiative](https://d3.harvard.edu/platform-digit/submission/a-closer-look-at-south-koreas-beloved-neighborhood-market-platform/index.html); [KoreaExperience guide](https://koreaexperience.com/blog/how-to-use-karrot-danggeun-korea-favorite-second-hand-app-2026)
- Xianyu: listing in ~30 seconds, voice descriptions, barcode scanning, photo-based price estimate, resale directly from Taobao order history — [App Store CN](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id510909506); AI camera ~5 s — [Beijing News](https://m.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1774420829129188.html)
- Mercari: barcode listing "in as little as 1 minute" (scan a book/CD/game/cosmetics barcode to auto-fill product data) — [App Store JP](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/id667861049); AI listing support in 3 taps, on by default — [ECnomikata](https://ecnomikata.com/ecnews/ecmall/44437/); shipping method pre-selected in listing form so anonymity is automatic — [Mercari column](https://jp-news.mercari.com/contents/1029)
- Carousell: median listing <2 min after List with AI, goal <45 s; "Snap-Sell, Chat-Buy" positioning — [Google Cloud: Carousell](https://cloud.google.com/customers/carousell); CEO claims "three seconds" with AI — [TechNode Global](https://technode.global/2026/07/07/singapores-recommerce-platform-carousell-reaches-ebitda-profitability-as-revenue-hits-141m/)
- Mercari 2023 survey: entering product info is burdensome; users abandon when unsure how detailed the description should be — [BCN Retail](https://www.bcnretail.com/topics_detail8/20240911_453500.html)
- Mercari Hallo (gig work, 2024–Dec 2025) was an attempt to extend the "list in seconds" idea to labour; discontinued 18 Dec 2025 — [FilingReader](https://filingreader.com/news-wire/tokyo/2025-11-07/mercaris-q1-2026-profits-soar-discontinues-hallo-service)

### Inferences
- The common pattern: (1) camera opens first; (2) category/title/price are suggestions, not required fields; (3) a short structured chip set replaces free-text (condition, negotiable, meet-up/parcel); (4) barcode lookup for media/cosmetics; (5) re-list/resell from order history. A Slovak/Czech portal competing with Bazoš's form-heavy flow could target <45 s like Carousell.

### Gaps
- No platform publishes measured median listing times other than Carousell's (<2 min); Karrot's "<30 s" and Xianyu's "30 s" are marketing claims.
