Pieniny National Park and the Dunajec gorge

Photo: Rj1979, Wikimedia Commons, public domain

Pieniny is a national park on the Slovak–Polish border where the Dunajec river cuts a gorge beneath the Three Crowns peaks. It is one of the most popular day trips in the whole of Slovakia.

  • Traditional wooden raft trips down the Dunajec gorge
  • Cycle path along the river between the two countries
  • Červený Kláštor monastery and village
  • Hiking to the Three Crowns and Sokolica viewpoints
