The investor's return has two parts: the monthly rental payout, about 2.8% of the price a year, and the growth in the value of the apartment. With value growth of 3% a year the total gain after ten years is about €131,000, an average of 6.2% a year; at 2% it is 5.0% and at 5% it is 9.1%. The Eurostat average for Slovakia in 2015 to 2024 was about 7.8% a year, but 2023 showed that prices can fall. Present this as a scenario, never as a promise.
