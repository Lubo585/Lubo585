Ľubovňa Castle, the landmark above the town

Photo: Kateryna Baiduzha, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

Ľubovniansky hrad is a 14th-century royal castle on a limestone hill above Stará Ľubovňa. It once guarded the Polish crown jewels and today it is one of the most visited monuments in eastern Slovakia.

  • Castle museum, towers and seasonal events
  • Open-air museum of folk architecture at its foot
  • Walking and cycling routes from the town
  • Visible from the site across the town
