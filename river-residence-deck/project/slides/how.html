How it works

From investment to monthly income in four steps

1

Invest from €200,000

Choose apartments in River Residence. Purchase contracts are signed with the developer and funds are held in escrow until completion.

2

Residence application

Our legal team structures the investment and prepares the residence permit application for the investor and eligible family members.

3

Fund lets the apartments

The management fund finds Slovak tenants, signs long-term leases, maintains the apartments and collects the rent.

4

Monthly dividend

Net rental income is transferred every month. The investor receives annual statements and keeps full ownership of the apartments.

The investor remains the registered owner of the apartments in the Slovak land registry at all times. The apartments can be sold, inherited or occupied by the owner's family later on.
