# Západná Európa, DACH a Benelux: Leboncoin, Kleinanzeigen, Marktplaats/2dehands, willhaben, tutti.ch, Ricardo.ch

Scope note: profiles of six general C2C classifieds portals as benchmark for Tržko (trzko.sk) vs. Bazoš.sk/Bazoš.cz. State as of 2025–2026 where sources allow; older facts are dated. Several official help centres (tutti.ch, willhaben, Ricardo, leboncoin help, ricardo.ch/gebuehren) returned 403/DNS errors in this environment, so some exact tariffs come from reputable secondary sources and are flagged. Note: a few cited articles carry 2026 publication dates (e.g. monsterdealz Aug 2026, lefrugalisme Sep 2026, t3n Jul 2026, Beobachter Jun 2026) and reflect the state at that time.

## Portal profiles: owner, history, scale, revenue

### Takeaway
All six are owned by large marketplace groups (Adevinta for FR/DE/NL-BE, Styria + Sprints Capital for AT, listed SMG for CH) and each is the dominant general classifieds brand in its country, with 3.8M (AT) to ~36–42M (DE) monthly users; precise 2025 revenue per portal is not public because Adevinta went private in 2024, but SMG (tutti/Ricardo) reports segment numbers.

### Inferences
- Scale gap vs. Slovakia/Czechia is enormous (Leboncoin 67–89M listings, Kleinanzeigen >58M, Marktplaats ~19M, willhaben 13M); the relevant lesson for Tržko is per-capita penetration: willhaben reaches 3.8M monthly users in a 9M-population country, i.e. ~40% of the population, on a free-private-listing model.
- All three Adevinta portals are moving the P&L from advertising toward transactional revenue (payments, shipping), as shown by Q1 2024 (+31% transactional vs −6% advertising) and Ricardo's take-rate growth via MoneyGuard.

## What each portal does better than a plain classifieds board like Bazoš (product features, UX, trust)

### Takeaway
Compared with Bazoš (free, phone-number-based, no payments, no verification), the six portals differentiate on four layers: (1) in-platform chat with scam filtering and no public phone numbers, (2) integrated escrow payment + shipping labels + buyer protection, (3) ratings/verification and fraud tooling, and (4) AI-assisted listing creation and conversational/image search; app quality is high (leboncoin iOS 4.7/5 from 1.6M ratings) while Trustpilot scores are low for the Adevinta/willhaben portals, driven mainly by support and account-block complaints rather than core product.

### Cited Findings
- Kleinanzeigen: "Sicher bezahlen" runs inside the message thread; funds held by Adyen (licensed by De Nederlandsche Bank) until buyer confirms receipt or Kleinanzeigen decides a dispute; DHL and Hermes labels generated on platform with tracking; PayPal being rolled out gradually as a payment method — [Kleinanzeigen Hilfe: Was ist Sicher bezahlen](https://hilfe.kleinanzeigen.de/hc/de/articles/17211553583388-Was-ist-Sicher-bezahlen-wie-funktioniert-der-K%C3%A4uferschutz); [Kleinanzeigen Hilfe: Lieferung](https://hilfe.kleinanzeigen.de/hc/de/articles/17212925050780-Wie-viel-kostet-die-Lieferung-und-was-sind-die-Lieferzeiten-bei-Sicher-bezahlen)
- Kleinanzeigen help centre lists a "Lösungscenter" (dispute resolution centre with status tracking of problem reports), refund tracking, phishing guides, "KA Pur" ad-free subscription, and separate paid packages for private vehicle and real-estate ads — [Kleinanzeigen Hilfe home](https://hilfe.kleinanzeigen.de/hc/de)
- Kleinanzeigen app got dark mode (May 2023); accounts, chat histories, ratings and ads carried over through the rebrand — [onlinemarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/unternehmensnews/kleinanzeigen-ohne-ebay-was-sich-aendert)
- Kleinanzeigen conversational search in ChatGPT (Feb 2026): natural-language queries incl. vague/combined requirements, product features, location; only search terms/location are sent, account data and messages stay on platform — [mobiflip, 25 Feb 2026](https://www.mobiflip.de/kleinanzeigen-jetzt-in-chatgpt-so-veraendert-ki-die-suche/)
- Leboncoin: image search ("photograph an object and check availability", compared to Google Lens), personalised homepage, AI listing from photo + title, price suggestion, delivery-mode recommendation; AI rollout started with "Jeux et Jouets", "Jeux vidéo", "Électroménager" (Dec 2024) — [Journal du Geek](https://www.journaldugeek.com/2024/12/17/leboncoin-se-met-a-lia-et-ca-va-tout-changer/)
- Leboncoin in ChatGPT (Feb 2026): connect account, describe need, results in carousel, refine by budget, ChatGPT compares listings; read-only access to listings, no access to private messages; transactions completed on leboncoin; first French platform with this integration — [Presse-citron](https://www.presse-citron.net/chatgpt-peut-desormais-fouiller-leboncoin-a-votre-place-et-ca-change-tout/)
- Leboncoin App Store reviews: praise for buyer-seller chat; a 2017 reviewer asked for a seller rating system (so ratings came later); complaints about ads, deleted-listing placeholders in purchase history, and (2025, pro seller) listing removals and fee increases — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/id484115113)
- Marktplaats: "Direct kopen" (May 2024) = fixed price, buyer pays immediately without chat, item reserved and shipped via DHL or PostNL; Kopersbescherming mandatory (5% on top of asking price), payment only via iDEAL payment request through Marktplaats; seller paid within 7 days; negotiation still possible in parallel — [Radar, 3 May 2024](https://radar.avrotros.nl/artikel/onderhandelen-op-marktplaats-verleden-tijd-zo-werkt-direct-kopen-60527)
- Marktplaats visibility products: Dagtopper (fixed spot above regular listings for 1/3/7 days, "up to 25x more views"), Direct omhoog (bump to top, "up to 2x views"), Homepagina (3 days on homepage feed + category page), Fotoknaller (two extra photos in search results), "Moet nu weg" sticker (7 days); prices differ per category and are shown in the ad flow; activation within 10 minutes — [marktplaats.nl/m/opvallen](https://www.marktplaats.nl/m/opvallen/)
- Marktplaats AI "Supersnel je advertentie maken": upload photo(s), tap "Maak mijn advertentie", AI fills title, description, details and category (Gemini gemini-2.0-flash-001), user sets price or allows bids; app only; excluded categories: cars, contacts, services, animals, houses/rooms, tickets, jobs, holidays, misc — [marktplaats.nl](https://www.marktplaats.nl/m/supersnel-je-advertentie-maken/)
- willhaben PayLivery (launched 28 July 2020, after silent launch): seller chooses "Versand" instead of "Abholung"; payment by credit card or Sofortüberweisung via Adyen; shipping via Österreichische Post or DPD with printable label; seller must hand over within 7 days or buyer is refunded; private-to-private only; item limit €999.99 at launch; seller must provide name, IBAN, address, email, phone, DOB — [Trending Topics](https://www.trendingtopics.eu/paylivery-willhaben-startet-payment-und-versand-optionen/)
- PayLivery is available exclusively in the willhaben chat; any offer to run PayLivery via WhatsApp/SMS is a scam; Austria-only shipping — search summary of [mimikama](https://www.mimikama.org/?p=319585) and [eurosender guide](https://www.eurosender.com/de/verkaufsleitfaden/willhaben)
- tutti.ch: internal chat is monitored, suspicious conversations filtered and blocked; advice to stay in chat (not WhatsApp/SMS/email), not click links/QR codes, personal pickup (cash against goods) for high-value items; reverse image search recommended against AI-generated fake photos; customer service can block accounts — [Beobachter, Jun 2026](https://www.beobachter.ch/konsum/zurcherin-macht-alles-richtig-und-verliert-trotzdem-941605)
- tutti.ch Trustpilot praise: easy listing creation, clear layout, listings are checked before release; complaints: scams, weak buyer protection/secure payment ("Käuferschutz nicht existent" vs Ricardo), wrongly deleted listings, pressure toward paid packages, no phone hotline, weak search filters (e.g. iPhone models) — [Trustpilot tutti.ch](https://ch.trustpilot.com/review/tutti.ch)
- Ricardo AI in app: tap AI icon and point camera at an item to see similar listings; when selling, AI generates descriptions from a photo; security measures: MoneyGuard, multi-factor authentication, passwordless login — [Blick interview, Nov 2024](https://www.blick.ch/wirtschaft/swiss-marketplace-chef-christoph-tonini-ueber-die-wichtigsten-schweizer-online-marktplaetze-wir-stehen-im-harten-wettbewerb-mit-giganten-id20326320.html)
- Ricardo is testing a new internal messaging tool that hides buyers' and sellers' contact details (reviewer mention) — [Trustpilot ricardo.ch](https://ch.trustpilot.com/review/ricardo.ch)
- SMG real-estate platforms (same group as tutti/Ricardo) launched AI property descriptions and two-factor authentication in H1 2025 — [SMG H1 2025](https://swissmarketplace.group/media-release/hyr-2025/)

### Inferences
- The decisive step beyond Bazoš is the "chat + escrow + label" bundle: it moves the phone number out of the listing, creates a closed loop in which fraud can be detected, and produces transactional revenue. All six portals (tutti.ch excepted, see gaps) have it.
- Conversational search via ChatGPT apps (Leboncoin Feb 2026, Kleinanzeigen Feb 2026) is now a benchmark feature at leaders; it is cheap to replicate for a smaller portal via an MCP/API connector.
- Ratings systems exist on Kleinanzeigen (ratings carried over in rebrand) and are being asked for/used elsewhere; exact mechanics were not retrievable (gap).

### Gaps
- Could not fetch official help pages for willhaben (hilfe.willhaben.at blocked), tutti.ch (support.tutti.ch not resolvable; tutti.ch/de/sicher-bezahlen 403), Ricardo (help.ricardo.ch and ricardo.ch/gebuehren 403), so exact descriptions of saved searches/alerts, radius search, phone-number handling, verification badges and rating mechanics per portal are not documented here with primary sources.
- Google Play ratings could not be extracted (pages truncated/404); only leboncoin iOS rating (4.7/5, 1.6M ratings) is documented.

## Secure payment / buyer protection / shipping: concrete mechanics and pricing

### Takeaway
The dominant pattern is buyer-paid escrow at roughly 4.5–5% + a fixed €0.40–0.70 fee, free for the seller, with money held by a licensed PSP (Adyen for Kleinanzeigen, Leboncoin, willhaben; OPP for Marktplaats) until the buyer confirms receipt within a short window (48 h at Marktplaats, 3 days at Leboncoin, ~10 days from shipping at Kleinanzeigen), plus platform-generated carrier labels; Ricardo is the outlier with a seller commission (8–12%, cap CHF 290) plus a 2–5% buyer MoneyGuard fee that became mandatory for shipped fixed-price items under CHF 1,500 in 2025.

### Cited Findings

**Kleinanzeigen "Sicher bezahlen" (DE)**
- Service fee 0.50 € + 4.5% of purchase price, paid by buyer; sellers pay nothing; PSP Adyen; covers never shipped, significantly different, counterfeit, wrong/incomplete; excludes accepted items/released payouts, installed/repaired items, digital goods, perishables, normal wear, minor deviations, non-original packaging, shipments abroad, drop-off/neighbour delivery; refund rules: full item price if not shipped (Hermes label refunded, DHL label cost not), item price but not return shipping for significant deviation — [Kleinanzeigen Hilfe](https://hilfe.kleinanzeigen.de/hc/de/articles/17211553583388-Was-ist-Sicher-bezahlen-wie-funktioniert-der-K%C3%A4uferschutz)
- Shipping (buyer pays, Germany only): DHL Paket S 2 kg €6.19, M 5 kg €7.69, L 10 kg €10.49, XL 31.5 kg €18.99; Hermes (25 kg) Päckchen €3.70 PaketShop / €4.95 online, S €4.50/€6.45, M €5.90/€7.95, L €9.90/€11.95; DHL delivery 1–2 working days — [Kleinanzeigen Hilfe Lieferung](https://hilfe.kleinanzeigen.de/hc/de/articles/17212925050780-Wie-viel-kostet-die-Lieferung-und-was-sind-die-Lieferzeiten-bei-Sicher-bezahlen)
- Deadlines: seller must deliver within 14 days after confirming availability; buyer has 10 days from shipping to confirm or report, and 3 days from delivery to confirm item is OK; once released, protection ends; Hermes Shop2Shop promotional price €0.99 until end of 2026 while quota lasts; examples: €2.75 fee on €50 item, €9.50 on €200 — [monsterdealz, Aug 2026](https://www.monsterdealz.de/magazin/kleinanzeigen-sicher-bezahlen)
- Older/other figures: e-recht24 cites €0.35 + 4.5% (older state); limit raised to €2,000 at some point (iphone-ticker) — search summaries of [e-recht24](https://www.e-recht24.de/ecommerce/13270-ebay-kleinanzeigen-kaeuferschutz.html) and [iphone-ticker](https://www.iphone-ticker.de/direkt-kaufen-ebay-kleinanzeigen-fuehrt-neue-funktion-ein-198917/); the current maximum amount is not stated on the official page (gap)
- Hermes shipping promotion: "bis Ende August Hermes-Versand bei Kleinanzeigen kostet jetzt die Hälfte, schon ab 1,99 €" (mydealz headline) — [mydealz](https://www.mydealz.de/magazin/bis-ende-august-hermes-versand-bei-kleinanzeigen-kostet-jetzt-die-haelfte-schon-ab-199e-61141)

**Leboncoin "paiement sécurisé" (FR)**
- CGV define "Frais de Service (Transaction sécurisée)" charged by LBC France to buyers or sellers, shown before finalising — [leboncoin CGV](https://leboncoin.fr/dc/cgv)
- Buyer fee: 0.70 € + 5% of price for delivery; hand delivery ("remise en main propre") capped at 1.99 €; private seller pays nothing; funds held by PSP until sale validated; buyer has 3 days after receipt to report a problem, otherwise automatic closure within max 30 days; hand-delivery card payments reach seller's "porte-monnaie leboncoin" in ~48 working hours; wallet-to-bank transfer takes a few days; instalments 3x/4x via Oney on eligible amounts — [lefrugalisme, updated 5 Sep 2026](https://www.lefrugalisme.com/paiement-securise-leboncoin/) (secondary source)
- Earlier rate (2021, quoting leboncoin help at the time): 4% of item price excl. shipping, minimum €0.99 — search summary of [Que Choisir forum](https://forum.quechoisir.org/paiement-securise-leboncoin-arnaque-t308856.html); a 2025 user reported €100 fee on a €2,500 hand-delivered transaction (~4%) — search summary of [Que Choisir forum](https://forum.quechoisir.org/leboncoin-paiement-securise-en-mains-propres-t365136.html). ## Documented weaknesses and user complaints

### Takeaway
