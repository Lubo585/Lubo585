The site

Levočská 11: between the street and the river

Address

Levočská 345/11, 064 01 Stará Ľubovňa. Parcels 775/1 to 775/5, title deed no. 1382

Setting

Southern part of the town, a few minutes from the main square, bounded by Levočská street to the north and the Jakubianka river to the south

Today

Brownfield of the former town bakery, now outdated warehouses. Redevelopment removes an eyesore and gives the town a new residential quarter

Ownership and access

All parcels owned by the developer. Vehicle access from Levočská street; planned footbridge over the Jakubianka to the riverside cycle path
