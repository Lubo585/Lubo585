Architecture

Calm, light facades and green roofs

  • Four buildings in a unified language, with light tones required by the town's colour rules
  • Prefabricated concrete wall system above a monolithic skeleton for speed and quality
  • Extensive green roofs on every building
  • Entrance level opened to parks, gardens and leisure areas
  • Two underground garage levels; vehicles stay out of the courtyard
  • Every apartment comes with its own storage unit

Design: Harajčík Architekti s.r.o., Trnava. Architectural and urban study, March 2025.
