The town and the rental market

Stará Ľubovňa: a district town with steady demand

Stará Ľubovňa is the administrative centre of its district in the Prešov Region, with about 16,000 inhabitants, a historic main square, a hospital, primary and secondary schools and a full range of shops and services.

  • Tenant demand from families, young professionals and hospital, school and public-sector staff
  • Cross-border economy with Poland twelve kilometres away
  • Tourism all year round: castle, spas, Pieniny and the Tatras
  • New-build 3-room apartments with parking and services on site are rare in the town; expected gross rent about €700 a month
