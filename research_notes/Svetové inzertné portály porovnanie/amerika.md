Research date: 29 October 2026. Reflects state 2025–2026. Every number is dated and sourced inline. Where sources conflict, both are given. Note on method: Meta, Craigslist (private), OfferUp (private) and Kijiji/Adevinta (private since May 2024) publish few hard numbers; many figures below are therefore from trade press (Value Added Resource, AIM Group via JobBoardHive), Similarweb estimates, or company help centres. ## Key question 1: Why has Facebook Marketplace beaten dedicated classifieds in many markets, and what are its documented weaknesses (scams, no escrow in most countries, poor search)?

### Takeaway
Marketplace won on distribution (bolted onto ~3 bn Facebook users, free listings, Messenger chat, "know your seller" identity), mobile-first UX, and later AI tooling (March 2026: AI-drafted listings, price suggestion, AI auto-replies, AI seller summaries, prepaid labels). Its documented weaknesses are an exceptionally high scam load (FTC: social-media scams $2.1 bn in 2025; UK bank TSB judged 34 % of sampled Marketplace ads to be scams and said Marketplace was 73 % of its purchase-fraud cases), no escrow/checkout outside the US (local pickup = cash, no protection), an algorithmic feed that users find hard to search, and weak radius/saved-search reliability.

### Cited Findings

**Owner, history, scale**
- Facebook Marketplace was (re)launched in 2016; the original 2007 version was handed to Oodle in 2009 and folded in 2014 — [Consultancy.uk / OC&C, 16 Apr 2021](https://www.consultancy.uk/news/27628/facebook-marketplace-disrupts-classifieds-ads-landscape)
- Meta does not publish a Marketplace MAU; its recent disclosures count listings. Meta Newsroom (March 2026): "more than 3.5 million listings are posted on Marketplace daily in the US and Canada" — [Meta Newsroom, 12 Mar 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/facebook-marketplace-new-meta-ai-tools-make-selling-faster-and-easier/)
- Secondary summary (SQ Magazine, citing Meta Newsroom 2026 and Meta Q2 2026 SEC filing): c. 430 million items listed globally per month incl. c. 44 million vehicles (Meta, July 2026); >200 million US fashion listings (Nov 2025); 1 in 3 US young adults on Facebook use Marketplace daily (July 2026); the older "1 billion monthly users" figure (first reported Q1 2021) has been retired by Meta — [SQ Magazine, 2026](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/facebook-marketplace-statistics/). I could not open the primary Meta page; treat the 430 M figure as secondary.
- Third-party aggregators still repeat 1.0–1.2 bn monthly Marketplace users for 2025 (e.g. [Coupert](https://www.coupert.com/research/facebook-marketplace-statistics), [Business of Apps citing TechCrunch 800 M](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/facebook-statistics/)); these are not Meta-reported and conflict with each other.
- Core Facebook MAU 3.07 bn in Q4 2025; Meta 2025 revenue c. $200.9–201 bn (no Marketplace breakout) — [Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/facebook-statistics/); [Sci-Tech Today](https://www.sci-tech-today.com/stats/facebook-marketplace-statistics/)
- Dwell: Marketplace "now easily outpacing Craigslist"; Statista cited at c. 142 M Craigslist monthly actives vs "billion-plus" for Marketplace — [Dwell](https://www.dwell.com/article/why-is-faceboook-marketplace-hard-to-use-6286ca6a)

**Why it won (documented reasons)**
- OC&C (2021): Facebook's edge = huge global user base, free C2C listings, cheaper professional listings, data that lets it "know your seller", >50 automotive partners and 9 rental/housing partners in US/Canada, on-platform transactions. Craigslist's monthly visits fell at a 24 % CAGR after Marketplace's late-2016 relaunch, attributed partly to Craigslist's slow interface upgrades and late mobile app (late 2019) — [Consultancy.uk / OC&C, 16 Apr 2021](https://www.consultancy.uk/news/27628/facebook-marketplace-disrupts-classifieds-ads-landscape)
- OC&C also found an incumbent can hold: Marktplaats (NL) remained first choice for ~7 in 10 Dutch buyers vs 9 % for Facebook Marketplace, thanks to its app and proprietary ad platform — same source.
- Dealhunter.io: Marketplace integrates with Messenger and the existing social graph; Messenger chat beats Craigslist's anonymised e-mail relay; Craigslist's "high-profile scams and crimes scared users away" and cash P2P had "zero recourse" — [Dealhunter.io](https://dealhunter.io/blog/craigslist-vs-modern-marketplaces)
- Canadian seller anecdotes: same item got 236 clicks/3 responses on Facebook vs 13 clicks/0 responses on Kijiji; Kijiji "very much about paying for Top ads" — [GuitarsCanada forum](https://www.guitarscanada.com/threads/it-seems-facebook-marketplace-has-greatly-superseded-kijiji-as-the-more-efficient-place-to-list-items-for-sale.310649/) (anecdotal)

**Business model and pricing**
- Local-pickup listings and sales are free (0 %); shipped checkout sales carry a selling fee. Sources conflict on the rate: Voolist (2026) says 5 % of item price excl. shipping, min $0.40 on items ≤ $8, "no fee increases announced" — [Voolist](https://www.voolist.com/blog/facebook-marketplace-fees-2026); Super-DS and several 2025–26 guides say the fee rose to 10 % with $0.80 minimum in April 2024 — [super-ds.com](https://super-ds.com/blog/facebook-marketplace-shipping-guide-2026); Growthscribe still lists 5 %/$0.40 — [Growthscribe](https://growthscribe.com/does-facebook-marketplace-charge-fees/). None of these is a Meta page (Meta help pages returned 403 to fetch). The fee is described as covering payment processing and Purchase Protection — [Accio](https://www.accio.com/blog/facebook-marketplace-selling-fees-all-you-need-to-know)
- Payouts for shipped orders arrive 1–5 business days after buyer confirms delivery or 15 days after tracking shows delivery — [Voolist](https://www.voolist.com/blog/facebook-marketplace-fees-2026)
- Shipped checkout is limited to US sellers (continental US); in UK, Canada, Australia and EU Marketplace is local pickup only with no shipping/checkout option — [super-ds.com](https://super-ds.com/blog/facebook-marketplace-shipping-guide-2026)
- Business model per Dwell: "users are the people posting, buying and selling, while advertisers are the customers" (Marketplace monetised via Meta ads) — [Dwell](https://www.dwell.com/article/why-is-faceboook-marketplace-hard-to-use-6286ca6a)

**Core product features and AI (2024–2026)**
- 12 Mar 2026 Meta AI tools: upload photos → Meta AI drafts the listing, fills details, suggests a price from similar items listed nearby; AI auto-replies to availability questions using listing description, pickup location and price (seller can enable/preview/edit during listing creation); AI-generated seller profile summary at top of profile (time on Facebook, friend count, listing history, item types, ratings); sellers can offer shipping, generate prepaid labels "in a few clicks" and track shipped orders in one dashboard — [Meta Newsroom, 12 Mar 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/facebook-marketplace-new-meta-ai-tools-make-selling-faster-and-easier/); [TechCrunch, 12 Mar 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/12/facebook-marketplace-now-lets-meta-ai-respond-to-buyers-messages/)
- Earlier Meta AI integrations (Nov 2025): a tool helping buyers "ask the right questions" before purchase and AI insights for vehicle listings — [TechCrunch, 12 Mar 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/12/facebook-marketplace-now-lets-meta-ai-respond-to-buyers-messages/)
- Value Added Resource tested the AI listing flow: photo to live listing in roughly 30 seconds — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/meta-ai-listing-facebook-marketplace/)
- Saved searches / alerts: tap the bell next to "Filters" after a search ("Notify Me"); managed in a "Saved Searches" section with per-search toggles and e-mail notification options; distance filter via Filters ("within X miles"). Users report alerts are delayed/inconsistent and that listings far outside the set radius still appear — [NoypiGeeks how-to](https://www.noypigeeks.com/?p=145224); third-party Chrome extension "MarketClean: Facebook Marketplace Distance Filter" exists to fix radius — [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/haknlbeddmjimamnjdadamhpokgngbln); paid third-party alert apps charge e.g. $7.99/month — [Scout, 9to5Mac, Aug 2025](https://9to5mac.com/2025/08/02/indie-app-spotlight-scout-facebook-marketplace-tool/)

**Trust & safety, scams**
- FTC (Consumer Sentinel, April 2026): US social-media scam losses rose from $261 M (2020) to $2.1 bn (2025), ~8x; nearly 30 % of people who lost money to a scam in 2025 said it started on social media; >40 % of social-media scam losers said it began with an ad for something they ordered; shopping scams were the most-reported social-media scam type — [SQ Magazine summary of FTC](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/facebook-marketplace-statistics/); [Techlicious on FTC](https://www.techlicious.com/blog/ftc-facebook-scam-losses-2025/)
- FTC rental-scam analysis (Dec 2025): ~65,000 reports, ~$65 M losses since 2020; about half of rental scams in the 12 months to June 2025 started on Facebook vs 16 % on Craigslist — [SQ Magazine summary](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/facebook-marketplace-statistics/)
- TSB (UK bank), 18 Jan 2024: fraud team sampled 100 Marketplace ads (cars, watches, consoles, air fryers, handbags), contacted sellers; 34 % judged scams; Marketplace = 73 % of all purchase-fraud cases at TSB — [Retail Banker International](https://www.retailbankerinternational.com/news/a-third-of-facebook-marketplace-adverts-could-be-scams/)
- US survey of 1,000 adults: 14 % report having been scammed on Marketplace; 79 % report seeing scams — [Cropink](https://cropink.com/fb-marketplace-statistics) (date unclear)
- BBB: red flags = overpayment, advance payment, moving off-Facebook, refusing viewing; advises meeting at police stations — [BBB tip](https://www.bbb.org/article/news-releases/29277-bbb-tip-consumers-and-businesses-beware-of-scams-on-facebook-marketplace); [41NBC/BBB Georgia](https://www.41nbc.com/facebook-marketplace-scams-bbb-safety-tips/)
- Typical scam patterns: overpayment/fake payment confirmations, fake "account upgrade" fee, Google Voice verification-code scam (FTC warning), copied rental listings — [Techlicious](https://www.techlicious.com/blog/ftc-facebook-scam-losses-2025/)
- Local cash sales have no purchase protection or dispute process; Purchase Protection applies only to shipped checkout — [Voolist](https://www.voolist.com/blog/facebook-marketplace-fees-2026)
- OC&C: Facebook's inventory depth/quality, seller services and lead quality trail dedicated classifieds, and parent-brand scandals make it less trusted — [Consultancy.uk / OC&C](https://www.consultancy.uk/news/27628/facebook-marketplace-disrupts-classifieds-ads-landscape)

### Inferences
- Marketplace's moat is distribution + identity, not product depth; a dedicated portal can still lead nationally (Marktplaats example) if it has a good app, a proprietary ad platform and better trust tooling.
- The gap Marketplace leaves open in non-US markets (incl. Slovakia/Czechia) is exactly escrow/checkout + shipping + buyer protection, plus reliable radius search and saved-search alerts — features a competitor like Tržko could own.
- Scam exposure is structural: free, unverified, high-volume listings with off-platform cash payment; verification (bank-ID like Kijiji/Interac, ID+selfie like OfferUp TruYou) and on-platform payment are the documented counters.

- The July 2026 "430 M listings/month" Meta figure is cited only via SQ Magazine; primary Meta page not retrieved.
- No Facebook "boost listing" price list could be verified (search budget exhausted).

## Key question 2: What does Craigslist still do right with its minimalist model, and where does it fail?

### Takeaway
Craigslist remains #1 US horizontal classifieds site by revenue and traffic (AIM Group 2025) with ~96–114 M monthly visits, extraordinary engagement (22–24 pages, 7 minutes per visit), no commission and only a few paid categories ($3–$75) — but revenue has collapsed ~70 % from $1.035 bn (2018) to ~$302 M (2024 est.), traffic falls ~10 %/month in late 2026 per Similarweb, it has no ratings, no verification, no payments, a late (2019) app and a reputation for scams.

### Cited Findings
- Founded 1995 as a San Francisco e-mail list; Craig Newmark held at least 42 % in 2017 (Forbes). 2017 Brookings-cited figures: 60 M monthly US users, 50 bn monthly pageviews, 80 M ads/month, 700+ local sites, 70 countries, 13 languages — [Expanded Ramblings](https://expandedramblings.com/index.php/craigslist-statistics/)
- Revenue (AIM Group estimates, proprietary methodology since 2003): 2018 $1.035 bn; 2021 $660 M; 2024 projected $302 M (−20 % YoY, −70 % since 2018). 2024 revenue mix: jobs 35.6 %, services 31 %, autos 20.3 %. AIM ranks Craigslist #1 in revenue and traffic among US horizontal classifieds, Mercari #2, OfferUp #3 — [JobBoardHive on AIM Group, 3 Apr 2025](https://www.jobboardhive.com/2025/04/03/craigslist-revenue-traffic-drops-again-one-third-of-2018-total/). Similarweb's company profile brackets revenue at $500 M–$1 bn (loose) — [Similarweb company](https://www.similarweb.com/company/craigslist.org/)
- Traffic: Similarweb Jan 2026: 113.8 M visits, 24.06 pages/visit, 7:04 min — [Expanded Ramblings](https://expandedramblings.com/index.php/craigslist-statistics/); Semrush Oct 2025: 112.42 M visits — [Semrush](https://cw.semrush.com/website/craigslist.org/overview); Similarweb Sept 2026: 96.2 M visits (3-month figure, −10.97 % MoM), global rank #161, US rank #49, 7:00 min, 22.94 pages/visit, bounce 22.49 %, US 94.18 % / Canada 3.92 % of traffic — [Similarweb craigslist.org](https://www.similarweb.com/website/craigslist.org/)
- Pricing (official): all postings free except: jobs (US, parts of Canada) $10–75 by area; apartment rentals in Boston/Chicago/NYC $5; commercial real estate $5; all for-sale-by-dealer categories $5; cars/trucks by dealer $5 (US, Vancouver); furniture by dealer Vancouver $5; cars/trucks/RVs/motorcycles by owner (US) $5; gigs $3–10; services $5 — [craigslist posting fees](https://www.craigslist.org/about/help/posting_fees)
- Model: "tiny staff, no spending on marketing or advertising, no sales team" (AIM Group) — [JobBoardHive](https://www.jobboardhive.com/2025/04/03/craigslist-revenue-traffic-drops-again-one-third-of-2018-total/)
- Failures: slow interface upgrades, mobile app only in late 2019; visits down 24 % CAGR after Marketplace relaunch — [Consultancy.uk / OC&C](https://www.consultancy.uk/news/27628/facebook-marketplace-disrupts-classifieds-ads-landscape); anonymised e-mail relay vs Messenger; cash P2P with zero recourse — [Dealhunter.io](https://dealhunter.io/blog/craigslist-vs-modern-marketplaces)
- 2026 safety review: no built-in ratings or reputation system, "weak" security, trust score 55/100 — [AtopLegal](https://atoplegal.com/review-craigslist/); persistent overpayment/fake-check and Google Voice code scams — [AmeriFreight 2026](https://www.amerifreight.net/information/craigslist-review-pros-cons-safety-tips)
- Design critics: outdated UI "makes it harder to spot scams and facilitates illegal activity, from fake rental listings to shady services"; Newmark favours simplicity — [WebDesignerDepot](https://webdesignerdepot.com/will-craigslist-ever-get-a-redesign-the-internets-most-stubborn-dinosaur/)
- Despite almost no change, the site still draws 114–143 M monthly visitors (2026) — [AmeriFreight](https://www.amerifreight.net/information/craigslist-review-pros-cons-safety-tips)
- FTC: 16 % of rental scams (12 months to June 2025) started on Craigslist vs ~50 % on Facebook — [SQ Magazine summary of FTC](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/facebook-marketplace-statistics/)
- App store ratings (third-party roundup, approximate): Craigslist iOS 4.8 / Play 4.7 — [Apurple](https://www.apurple.co/apps-like-offerup/)

### Inferences
- Craigslist "does right": zero friction (no account needed for most posts, no commission, no algorithmic feed), deep engagement, trusted for jobs/services/housing where it still monetises, and e-mail relay that hides the phone number.
- It "fails" on: identity/trust, mobile product, messaging, payments, discovery — and it is losing both traffic and revenue every year, so minimalism alone is no longer a defensible strategy.

### Gaps
- No audited Craigslist revenue; all figures are AIM Group estimates (AIM's 2025 report costs $495 and was not accessed). No official live-listing count for 2025–26.

## Key question 3: How do OfferUp and Mercari combine local pickup with shipping and payments, and what do they charge?

### Takeaway
They have diverged: OfferUp killed nationwide shipping (new listings 23 Sept 2025, all listings 21 Oct 2025) and is now local-only, free, with no in-app payments, monetised via Promote/Promote Plus ($19.99/month or $99.99/year), paid listings above free category limits, and a $7.99/month buyer "Premium". Mercari US killed its Uber-based "Mercari Local" delivery (Nov 2024), is now a pure shipping marketplace with full checkout, charging since 6 Jan 2025 a 10 % seller fee + 3.6 % buyer protection fee, no separate processing fee, free direct-deposit payout, and it returned to profit (¥0.9 bn FY2025, ¥2 bn FY2026) on $810 M GMV.

### Cited Findings

**OfferUp — owner, history, scale**
- Founded April 2011 by Nick Huzar and Arean Van Veelen, Bellevue WA; positioned as mobile-first Craigslist competitor; acquired Letgo (US) on 25 Mar 2020 (deal led by OLX Group, ~$120 M), apps merged 31 Aug 2020; total funding $381 M (Dec 2022); investors a16z, Warburg Pincus, GGV, Tiger Global, T. Rowe, Coatue; CEO Todd Dunlap (since 2021, replacing Huzar) — [Wikipedia OfferUp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OfferUp); [Lilach Bullock 2026](https://www.lilachbullock.com/is-offerup-legit/)
- Scale: >20 M MAU at Letgo merger (Mar 2020) — [PR Newswire, Mar 2020](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/offerup-and-letgo-combine-us-marketplaces-to-deliver-a-better-buying-and-selling-experience-for-more-than-20-million-monthly-users-301029334.html); ~20 M MAU, 260 employees (Sept 2021), ~90 M downloads (June 2021) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OfferUp); "40 M+ yearly users" (Apr 2024–Mar 2025, OfferUp 2025 Good Neighbor Index), 56 M yearly users (2022 report), 150 M+ downloads, 30 M+ annual transactions, "150 M+ community" (About Us, 2026), 2023 described by CEO as "profitable growth" — [Expanded Ramblings, updated 24 Feb 2026](https://expandedramblings.com/index.php/offerup-statistics-facts/). OfferUp does not publish a current MAU. Claims of "56 M MAU in 2024" on some blogs conflate yearly users with monthly.
- AIM Group ranks OfferUp #3 US horizontal classifieds by revenue/traffic (2025) — [JobBoardHive](https://www.jobboardhive.com/2025/04/03/craigslist-revenue-traffic-drops-again-one-third-of-2018-total/)

**OfferUp — shipping end, payments, pricing**
- Shipping option removed from new listings 23 Sept 2025 and from all remaining listings 21 Oct 2025; OfferUp help page (edited 22 Sept 2026) says it only supports local, in-person transactions and has no plans to bring shipping back; the former 12.9 % (min $1.99) shipping seller fee no longer applies — [Underpriced.app guide citing OfferUp help "Nationwide shipping ending"](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/offerup-selling-guide-2026); GeekWire reported a Dunlap memo on deprecating shipping in 2025 — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/is-offerup-legit/)
- No fee or commission on in-person sales; no in-app payment processing — buyers pay in person, "once payment is handed over, the sale is final"; no refunds through OfferUp — [Underpriced.app / OfferUp help](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/offerup-selling-guide-2026); [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/is-offerup-legit/)
- Free listing limits per month (reset on the 1st, checked 18 Oct 2026): Cars & Trucks 1, Motorcycles 1, Appliances 15, Cell Phones & Accessories 15, Furniture 25, Jewelry & Accessories 25, Tools & Machinery 25, 200 combined for all other categories; beyond limits: paid single listing or monthly package priced by category in-app — [Underpriced.app / OfferUp help "About free listing limits"](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/offerup-selling-guide-2026)
- Promote: 1-day or 3-day boost, stackable, price varies by item/category and shown in-app (one 2026 guide quotes ~$1.99/item/day, unconfirmed); Promote Plus: $19.99/month or $99.99/year, one item promoted indefinitely, swappable — [Underpriced.app](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/offerup-selling-guide-2026); [Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/beginner-guides-how-tos/how-to-sell-on-offerup)
- Premium: $7.99/month buyer subscription (not needed to sell) — [Underpriced.app](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/offerup-selling-guide-2026)
- Product: infinite-scroll photo feed, location-based results with category/price/distance filters, offers sortable by highest, "Bump", in-app messaging that hides e-mail/phone, profile badges, reviews, response times, keyword-based anti-fraud flagging — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OfferUp)
- App ratings (third-party, approximate): iOS 4.8, Play 4.4 — [Jotform roundup](https://jotform.com/blog/free-apps-for-buying-and-selling)

**Mercari US — owner, history, scale, results**
- Mercari Inc. (Tokyo, TSE 4385) launched the US marketplace in 2014. FY ends 30 June. FY2025 (to 30 Jun 2025): first-ever full-year US profit, core operating profit ¥900 M, after a "disastrous" 2024 fee change (fees shifted from sellers to buyers in March 2024) drove a buyer exodus, ~45 % of US staff laid off in 2024, and US CEO John Lagerling's resignation; founder Shintaro Yamada took over the US in Jan 2025 — [Value Added Resource, 6 Aug 2025](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/mercari-us-first-ever-full-year-profit/)
- US GMV by fiscal year: $1.015 bn (FY2023), $913 M (FY2024), $728 M (FY2025), $810 M (FY2026, +11 %). Q4 FY2026 (Apr–Jun 2026): GMV $208 M (+15 %), MAU 4.2 M (+4 %, first positive YoY after declines), revenue ¥10.4 bn (+21 %), core operating profit ¥400 M; FY2026 revenue ¥40.7 bn (+12 %), core operating profit ¥2 bn ("more than doubled", second consecutive profitable year). FY2027 target: ≥10 % US GMV growth at ~break-even; Jeff LeBeau became US CEO 1 July 2026 — [Value Added Resource, 10 Aug 2026](https://valueaddedresource.net/mercari-q4-2026-earnings/)
- Q2 FY2026 (Oct–Dec 2025): US GMV ~$196 M (+12 %), MAU 4.3 M (−4 % YoY); CFO calls MAU the key metric; growth attributed to "cumulative product improvements"; trading cards/collectibles lead, fashion recovering — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/mercari-us-gmv-up-user-growth-lags/)
- Mercari ranked #2 US horizontal classifieds by AIM Group (2025) — [JobBoardHive](https://www.jobboardhive.com/2025/04/03/craigslist-revenue-traffic-drops-again-one-third-of-2018-total/)

**Mercari US — pricing (official help centre, updated 23 Oct 2026)**
- Selling fee 10 % of item price + buyer-paid shipping (listings created/updated on or after 6 Jan 2025); Buyer Protection fee 3.6 % paid by buyer; no separate payment-processing fee for new/updated listings (legacy listings: buyer paid 10 % + $0.50 + 2.9 % until updated); Direct Deposit payout $0 (the $2 fee was removed 6 Jan 2025); Instant Pay $3 flat (max $600/month, once a day); frequent-cancellation fee 5 % capped at $25; Mercari Authenticate $5, Certificate $10 (seller-paid); prepaid labels USPS/UPS/FedEx up to 100 lb (e.g. USPS Ground Advantage 0.25 lb $4.91, rising to $5.39 from 19 Oct 2026); Media Mail $4.99/1 lb; freight >150 lb $2,500 flat — [Mercari help "Fees on Mercari"](https://www.mercari.com/us/help_center/article/169/); fee-change notice — [Mercari help 2517](https://www.mercari.com/us/help_center/article/2517/); context on reversal — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/mercari-backtracks-on-fee-changes/)
- Mercari Local (Uber-powered local delivery, launched 2021 as Mercari Now, nationwide with fees $15.99–$199 for bulky items) was abruptly discontinued in Nov 2024 with <24 h notice — [Value Added Resource, 21 Nov 2024](https://valueaddedresource.net/mercari-local-uber-delivery-discontinued); [Retail Dive on launch](https://www.retaildive.com/news/mercari-uber-expand-local-delivery-program/603848/)
- Seller tools: Smart Pricing (automatic gradual price drops to a seller-set floor, likers notified), Smart Offers (automatic one-time counteroffer within seller's range), time-limited sales, auto offers to likers, bundles, prepaid-label discounts, "best rate" shipping, Fashion Exchange returns pilot (July 2025) — [Nifty.ai](https://www.nifty.ai/post/mercari-smart-pricing); [QuickList AI](https://quicklistai.org/mercari-smart-pricing-guide/); [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/mercari-us-first-ever-full-year-profit/)
- AI: Mercari says 70 % of product-development code is AI-generated; smaller AI-assisted teams work on features, US UI/UX, customer service and fraud prevention; "improve the user experience and site security using AI tools" — [Yahoo Finance transcript Q4 FY2025](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/4385.T/earnings/4385.T-Q4-2025-earnings_call-313806.html); [Value Added Resource, Aug 2026](https://valueaddedresource.net/mercari-q4-2026-earnings/). I found no official Mercari US photo-to-listing AI generator; third-party tools (FlipList AI, Nifty) fill that niche — [FlipList AI](https://fliplistai.com/mercari-listing-generator)
- Mercari Global app (cross-border buying from Japan, launched Taiwan/HK late 2025, US 20 Jun 2026): real-time AI translation, duties shown at checkout (Zonos), Mercari handles payment/shipping/inspection, shipping protection — [Value Added Resource, 20 Jun 2026](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/mercari-opens-global-app-to-us/)
- App ratings (third-party, approximate): iOS 4.8, Play 4.7 — [Apurple](https://www.apurple.co/apps-like-offerup/)

### Inferences
- The two US "hybrid" models both abandoned the hybrid: OfferUp found shipping unprofitable/low-trust and retreated to free local; Mercari found local delivery unviable and doubled down on shipped checkout with fees on both sides. For Tržko this suggests: either integrate shipping+escrow deeply (Mercari/Vinted style, with a buyer-protection fee around 3–5 % plus seller fee) or stay local and monetise via promotion/limits — half-measures were cut.
- Mercari's 2024 experiment is a documented warning: shifting all fees to buyers at checkout produced cart abandonment and a GMV collapse (−28 % FY2023→FY2025); a split 10 % + 3.6 % restored growth.

## Key question 4: Which hyperlocal and safety features (safe trade spots, TruYou verification, ID checks) exist and work?

### Takeaway
OfferUp has the deepest safety stack (free voluntary TruYou ID+selfie via Onfido, ~1,960 Community MeetUp Spots with police/businesses, SafeTradeSpots.com since 2018, keyword scam flagging), Kijiji added bank-based Interac Verified (14 Apr 2026, free, ~2 min) with document/liveness ID expected later in 2026, Facebook relies on social-graph signals plus AI seller summaries (2026) and ratings, Nextdoor relies on address-verified neighbours (105 M+ verified) with no payments, and Craigslist has none. Evidence that these features reduce fraud is thin: every platform disclaims that a badge "doesn't guarantee" identity, and BBB/police still steer users to police-station exchanges.

### Cited Findings

**OfferUp**
- TruYou: free, voluntary; verify phone, upload two photos of US government ID (driver's licence, state ID or passport), live selfie; checks run by Onfido; OfferUp says it doesn't store the ID/selfie; usually minutes, up to 48 h; badge "doesn't guarantee the user is who they say they are"; separate badge for connecting Facebook — [Underpriced.app citing OfferUp help](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/offerup-selling-guide-2026); [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/is-offerup-legit/)

### Inferences
- The strongest 2025–26 trust trend in the Americas is bank/ID-backed identity badges that are free and voluntary (Kijiji-Interac, OfferUp-Onfido) rather than escrow; for a Slovak/Czech portal, a bank-ID (BankID-style) verification plus a published network of safe-exchange points (police stations) is the cheapest analogue.
- Hyperlocal pure-free models (Nextdoor Finds, OfferUp post-shipping, Kijiji) monetise through promotion, listing limits in commercial categories and display ads — not transaction fees — while shipped-checkout models (Mercari, FB US checkout) monetise 10–13.6 % of GMV.
