The investment proposition

Residence pathway

An investment of €200,000 or more can be structured to support an application for a Slovak residence permit, giving the investor and family a legal base in the European Union and the Schengen Area.

Monthly dividend

The apartments are let to Slovak tenants through our management fund. Net rental income is paid to the investor every month, in euros, into a bank account of their choice.

Real EU asset, fully managed

The investor owns newly built apartments in a Eurozone country. Tenants, leases, maintenance, rent collection and reporting are handled locally, so the investment is hands-off from day one.
