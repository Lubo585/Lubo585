Living on the Jakubianka

The Jakubianka flows along the southern edge of the site before joining the Poprad river in the town. Its banks give the quarter its name and its calm: greenery, water and a path for evening walks right below the windows.

  • Regulated channel designed for a 100-year flood, coordinated with the river authority
  • Existing cycle path on the opposite bank, linked by a planned footbridge
  • Riverside park and lawns as part of the development
  • Apartments on the river side look over water and the hills beyond
