Residence pathway

A legal base in the European Union

What the permit offers

  • Temporary residence in Slovakia, a member of the EU, the Schengen Area and the Eurozone
  • Travel within the Schengen Area for up to 90 days in any 180-day period
  • Family members can apply for residence through family reunification
  • Renewable, with a route to long-term residence after five years of continuous stay
  • Access to Slovak banking, healthcare and schools for the family

How we structure it

  • Investment of €200,000 or more into River Residence apartments
  • Holding structure set up by our legal team in Slovakia
  • Application prepared and filed at the Slovak embassy or consulate
  • Ongoing compliance, renewals and reporting handled locally

Important: Slovak law does not grant residence for a property purchase alone. Residence is obtained under the business-purpose permit, subject to individual eligibility and annual quotas. Each investor receives an independent legal assessment before committing funds.
