# Nordic and Central/Eastern European classifieds portals + the Slovak/Czech baseline (FINN, Blocket, DBA, Tori, OLX, Jófogás, Bazoš, Sbazar, Aukro, Vinted)

Research date: 29 October 2026. All figures are dated inline and attributed. Note on method: the web-search budget for this session was exhausted midway, so part of the evidence comes from direct fetches of official pages; several Zendesk-hosted help centres (hjelpesenter.finn.no, blocket.zendesk.com, support.dba.dk, tuki.tori.fi) and pomoc.olx.pl returned HTTP 403 / JS-only pages, which is why some exact fee tables are reported from search snippets or third-party pages and flagged accordingly.

## Key question 1: What do the Nordic portals (FINN.no, Blocket, DBA, Tori) do better — especially identity verification and integrated payment + shipping — and what do they charge?

### Takeaway
All four Nordic portals are owned by one listed company (Vend Marketplaces ASA, ex-Schibsted Marketplaces) and run the same playbook: free private listings in most categories, strong-ID verification (BankID on Blocket; MitID-validated profiles on DBA; Vipps/Adyen accounts on FINN), and an escrow-style "pay in app, ship with a national carrier, money released ~24 h after delivery" flow (FINN "Fiks ferdig", Blocket "Frakt med köpskydd" ex-"Blocketpaketet", DBA "Fiks færdig", Tori "ToriDiili") whose cost is a buyer-paid, price-tiered fee (roughly 20–700 SEK on Blocket; ~4.50 € on a 75 € item on Tori; a flat "Trygg betaling" fee on FINN) plus discounted carrier shipping. Monetisation of the group is ~70 % listing/publishing fees (mostly vehicles, property, jobs); Recommerce (general goods) is run at a loss (−32 % EBITDA margin) as a traffic driver.

**FINN.no (Norway)**
- Private Torget ads: FINN's help centre states private users can post ads for a year at a time free of charge; paid extras sold separately. A listing fee applies in the "car, boat and MC equipment" category: 29 kr for ads priced under 2,000 kr and 79 kr for ads over 2,000 kr; "Gi bort" (give-away) ads stay free; separate trailer prices (figures from the FINN help article as surfaced in search; the page itself returned 403 on fetch) — [FINN hjelpesenter: Annonsegebyr på FINN torget](https://hjelpesenter.finn.no/hc/no/articles/26389568788498-Annonsegebyr-p%C3%A5-FINN-torget)
- FINN's Torget rules: private advertising is only for private individuals; anyone reselling regularly or showing a business logo/store reference must use the business option — [FINN: Spesifikke regler for FINN Torget](https://www.finn.no/vilkar/spesifikke-regler-torget)
- Fiks ferdig terms (official): "Fraktpris og avgift for Trygg betaling dekkes av Kjøperen" (buyer pays shipping and the secure-payment fee); no seller fee listed; fee and shipping are not refunded once used even if the sale is reversed. Carriers: Posten, Helthjem, PostNord. Payment partner: Adyen N.V. — sellers must register as Adyen customers; funds held in a secure Adyen account once the seller accepts. Purchase request valid 48 h; seller has 7 working days to ship or the purchase is cancelled/refunded; buyer has 24 h after receipt to inspect and pause payment; if paused and nothing happens, released after 10 days. Claims only for material deviation (counterfeit, wrong product, undisclosed defects, wrong size, transport damage from poor packaging); no right of withdrawal. Compensation caps per package: 2,500 (Liten / Liten Pluss) and 10,000 (Medium/Stor/Ekstra stor) NOK. Dispute: report within 24 h → 10 days to agree → escalate to FINN customer service, which decides. Both parties need a Vend account — [FINN: Vilkår for Fiks ferdig](https://www.finn.no/vilkar/privat/torget-privat/fiksferdig)
- FINN's own Fiks ferdig explainer: seller ticks package size under "Fiks ferdig frakt og betaling"; after accepting a buyer, Helthjem provides shipping details; seller can leave the parcel on the doorstep/mailbox/drop-off point; payment reaches the seller via Vipps 24 hours after delivery; buyer pays with Vipps, amount charged immediately but held by FINN until Helthjem confirms delivery — [FINN: Fiks ferdig (artikkel)](https://www.finn.no/bap/artikler/aktuelt/fiks-ferdig)
- Fee level: FINN listings surfaced in search show a fee line "Frakt fra 39 kr + Trygg betaling 39 kr" and "ved kjøp via Fiks ferdig tilkommer gebyret de tar for tjenesten" (a flat buyer fee, not a percentage). A third-party blog claims FINN charges no Fiks ferdig fee from either party — this contradicts FINN's own terms (buyer pays "avgift for Trygg betaling") and the listing text, so treat the blog as wrong — [FINN listing example](https://www.finn.no/418457491); [Faktarommet blog (unreliable)](https://faktarommet.net/blogg/fiks-ferdig-pris-frakt/); [FINN terms](https://www.finn.no/vilkar/privat/torget-privat/fiksferdig)
- App: FINN.no on Google Play (developer Vend Marketplaces ASA, package no.finn.android): 4.1 stars, 23.3K reviews, 1M+ downloads; tagline "Lagre ditt drømmesøk, få varsel!" (saved search + alerts) — [Google Play search: finn.no](https://play.google.com/store/search?q=finn.no&c=apps&hl=en)

**Blocket (Sweden)**
- Service naming: Blocket's shipping service is now "Frakt med köpskydd", previously "Blocketpaketet"; it is voluntary and only shown in ads where the seller enabled shipping — [Säkerbetalning.nu on Blocket (reviewed 12 Aug 2026)](https://xn--skerbetalning-bfb.nu/saker-betalning-blocket)
- Identity: "Både köpare och säljare verifieras med mobilt BankID"; money is held by payment partner Adyen when a price proposal is accepted or "Köp nu" is used; proposal valid 48 h; seller has 7 working days to hand in the parcel, buyer 7 working days to collect; buyer has 24 h from pickup to pause payment ("Jag har problem med varan"); PostNord carries the parcel with tracking; cover is for material deviation only (no ångerrätt/open purchase); shipping insurance max 5,000 kr + shipping cost; uncollected parcels return at buyer's cost with no refund of price/shipping/köpskydd; disputes: 10 days to agree, Blocket's requests must be answered within 3 days, returns within 7 working days with Blocket's return label. For in-person deals ("Överlämning") Blocket recommends Swish/bank transfer/cash at handover, no köpskydd; for cars a BankID-signed digital purchase contract is recommended. Blocket states it never sends payment links by e-mail/SMS — [Säkerbetalning.nu on Blocket](https://xn--skerbetalning-bfb.nu/saker-betalning-blocket)

- Benchmark competitor Tradera (Sweden): seller commission 10 % (min 3 SEK, max 200 SEK); first 500 listings free; buyer protection 2 SEK + 5 % capped at 499 SEK for purchases from 200 SEK (watches cap 749 SEK since 13 Oct 2026); buyer protection became mandatory 7 Jan 2025 — [PC Concept](https://pc-concept.nu/basta-kop-och-salj-sajter/); [Flashback thread](https://www.flashback.org/p90305060)

**DBA (Denmark)**
- Fiks færdig on DBA: buyer pays through DBA; DBA releases payment to seller only after the buyer has received the parcel, automatically 24 h after delivery once the buyer has checked the item; QR code generated at purchase so the seller prints a label at a PostNord parcel shop (1,300+ shops); tracking in PostNord app; seller selects package size when listing. **Tori (Finland)**
- ToriDiili (Tori's payment + delivery service) was introduced in 2024; holds the buyer's money until the item is received; buyer pays item price + shipping + service fee; buying without ToriDiili remains possible. Yle's December 2024 test with a 75 € Marimekko bag: Tori listing free; buyer pays **4.50 € service fee + 2.99 € shipping** (7.49 € total); Vinted comparison: 0.70 € + 5 % (4.45 €); Tise: 1–5 % buyer protection + 2.69 € + 3 % seller protection — [Yle, 25 Dec 2024](https://yle.fi/a/74-20132308)

- A Google Play search for "tori.fi" did not surface the Tori app in the visible results; app rating not obtained — [Google Play search: tori.fi](https://play.google.com/store/search?q=tori.fi&c=apps&hl=en)

### Inferences
- The Nordic model is "free to list, pay to transact": the escrow fee is paid by the buyer, tiered by price, with a floor (~20 SEK / ~2–5 €) that makes it regressive on cheap items (26 % on a 100 kr item on Blocket) — exactly the complaint pattern a CZ/SK portal should expect if it copies it without a low-value tier.
- Strong ID (BankID/MitID/Vipps) is layered: verified profile badges for everyone, plus mandatory strong ID for anyone using the pay-and-ship flow; the escrow partner (Adyen) carries KYC, which also lets the portal decide disputes.
- Even with ~70 % of revenue from paid verticals, Vend runs general-goods Recommerce at a −32 % EBITDA margin; the escrow fee alone does not fund a general-goods marketplace — it funds trust and traffic for cars/property/jobs.
- Google Play ratings (Blocket 2.5, DBA 3.7, FINN 4.1) show that redesigns and mandatory flows generate backlash even at market leaders; App Store ratings are far higher (Blocket 4.4), so quoting one store only is misleading.

## Key question 2: How does OLX run free listings and still monetise, and what are OLX Delivery's mechanics and fees? (plus Jófogás as the Hungarian comparator)

### Takeaway
OLX Group (Prosus) earned US$777 m revenue and US$270 m aEBIT (35 % margin) in FY25 (year to 31 Mar 2025) from 9 brands in 9 markets with ~64 m live listings and 29 m monthly app users; growth comes from Motors (+24 %) and Real Estate (+23 %), i.e. paid listings/packages and dealer/agent tools, while general-goods listings are free up to per-subcategory monthly limits and monetised through bumps (12.99 zł), highlights (3.99–27.99 zł/7 days), business plans (99–499 zł/month) and advertising. "Przesyłka OLX" (OLX Delivery) is a PayU-escrow + locker-shipping flow (InPost 5 zł, Orlen Paczka 6 zł, Poczta Polska 8.50 zł, Aug 2024 price list) with a buyer-paid service fee capped at 50 zł and a "Pakiet Ochronny" refund guarantee — but Prosus said in mid-2025 it is "de-prioritising general goods pay-and-ship solutions in Europe".

### Cited Findings

**Owner, history, scale**
- OLX ("OnLine eXchange") founded 2006 by Fabrice Grinda and Alec Oxenford; Naspers took a majority stake in 2010 and 95 % in 2014; Christian Gisy named CEO April 2024 (Romain Voog 2021–2024). 2021 market list included Poland, Portugal, Romania, Ukraine, Bulgaria, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Brazil, India, Indonesia, South Africa etc.; India sold to CarTrade and Indonesia to Astra in 2023. Brands include OLX, Otodom, Otomoto, Autovit.ro, Standvirtual, Imovirtual, Fixly — [Wikipedia: OLX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OLX)
- FY2025 (to 31 Mar 2025): revenue US$777 m (+18 %); aEBIT US$270 m (+61 % local currency), 35 % margin (25 % in FY24); 9 brands in 9 markets; ~64 million active listings daily; 29 million monthly app users; Motors revenue +24 % (dealer tools, vehicle history reports, ratings); Real Estate +23 % (unified platform, redesigned app, agent tools); Jobs strengthened in Poland, Romania, Ukraine with AI matching; >US$17 m invested in AI, 55 AI use cases (search, moderation, fraud protection); guidance: >20 % growth, margins "approaching 50 %" — [OLX Group press release, 23 Jun 2025](https://www.olxgroup.com/news/olx-group-reports-strong-fy-2025-results-with-18-revenue-growth-and-61-profit-uplift-year-on-year/)
- Prosus FY25 commentary: OLX "de-prioritising its general goods pay-and-ship solutions in Europe", focusing on motors and real estate; OLX Brasil (50 % JV) margin fell to 2 % from 27 %, paying listers −6 % — [OnlineMarketplaces: Prosus FY25](https://www.onlinemarketplaces.com/articles/prosus-grows-real-estate-revenue-23-but-profits-fall-at-olx-brasil/)
- Ukraine: OLX bought Slando.ua in 2011, rebranded to OLX.ua on 15 Sep 2014 (4.5 m monthly visitors then); 2021: 18.5 million weekly users and 55 million monthly visits; delivery service launched May 2017 with UAPAY and Nova Poshta (buyer's card charged only after receipt). Sept 2025: Ukrainian government bill No. 14025 ("OLX tax") would make platforms withhold 23 % (or reduced 10 %) PIT on sales incl. used goods; OLX called it overly harsh — [Ukrainian Wikipedia: OLX](https://uk.wikipedia.org/wiki/OLX)
- Portugal: OLX.pt was initially run by FixeAds; became OLX Portugal (Naspers) in early 2018; sister sites Standvirtual (cars), Imovirtual (property), Coisas — [Portuguese Wikipedia: OLX](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/OLX)

**Przesyłka OLX / Płatności OLX (OLX Delivery) mechanics and fees**
- Flow: buyer filters "Dostępne z Przesyłką OLX", clicks "Kup", chooses delivery, pays; service includes Pakiet Ochronny, tracking and fast payment; if a listing lacks Przesyłka OLX the buyer can ask the seller to enable it. Pakiet Ochronny: report empty parcel / non-conforming item within 24 h of receipt; seller has 48 h to accept/reject a return; OLX decides within 30 days; 72-hour windows for buyer data, seller return-shipping payment and buyer dispatch; seller protected if buyer fails to return — [Spider's Web, 19 Jul 2024](https://spidersweb.pl/2024/07/przesylka-olx-jak-dziala.html)
- Carriers and standard shipping prices (Aug 2024 price list): InPost Paczkomat 24/7 S/M/L 5.00 zł; Orlen Paczka S/M/L 6.00 zł; Poczta Polska S/M/L 8.50 zł, XL 12.00 zł; periodic "Przesyłka za 1 zł" promotions (20 Aug 2024 across all categories) — [FashionBiznes, 20 Aug 2024](https://fashionbiznes.pl/olx-jakie-formy-przesylki-oferuje-kiedy-olx-wysylka-za-1-zl/)
- Payments and KYC: payments run by PayU; standard verification = name + PayU terms; selling with "Kup z Przesyłką" requires bank account number + date of birth; enhanced verification (ID photos with PESEL, address, refundable 1 PLN transfer) if a single sale >3,000 PLN, continuous sales, or cumulative 30,000 PLN; funds released after the buyer receives and confirms the parcel — [Tabletowo, 31 May 2022](https://www.tabletowo.pl/?p=663639)
- Buyer "opłata serwisowa": introduced with the 25 Oct 2021 terms; added to the item price and paid by the buyer when using Przesyłka OLX / Płatności OLX; maximum **50 zł**; amount shown in the app before the transaction; listings without OLX payments/shipping carry no fee — [Instalki, 12 Oct 2021](https://www.instalki.pl/news/internet/olx-wprowadza-nowe-prowizje-zaplaca-kupujacy-to-spory-krok-wstecz/)
- Pakiet Ochronny (24 Aug 2021 version): replaces "Bezpieczne Transakcje OLX"; payment paused up to 14 days on a report; compensation up to **3,000 PLN** (listing price or market price); buyer assigns claims against the seller to OLX — [Android.com.pl, 7 Aug 2021](https://android.com.pl/artykuly/423243-pakiet-ochronny-olx/)
- UOKiK decision 27 Feb 2024 (changes effective 14 Mar 2024): Pakiet Ochronny widened to cover damaged, wrong-colour/size, used-as-new and incomplete items (previous exclusions removed); listings must show two prices (item alone and with Przesyłka OLX); refunds for users whose claims were rejected since 24 Aug 2021 and for service fees charged since 1 Dec 2021; terms must state buyers can buy directly from the seller without the service fee — [Android.com.pl, 29 Feb 2024](https://android.com.pl/tech/695778-zakupy-na-olx-bezpieczniejsze/); [Android.com.pl on UOKiK refunds](https://android.com.pl/tech/717325-prezes-uokik-olx-zwrot-pieniedzy/)

### Inferences
- Head-on "better UX" attacks on Bazoš (Letgo, Hyperinzerce, Sbazar's 2018 and 2025 redesigns) have not moved share; Sbazar's visits fell from ~6 M (Mar 2025) to ~3.6 M (Sep 2026) even while it shipped Messenger, Bank iD verification and a new app — liquidity, not features, decides general classifieds.
- The challengers that grew did so by changing the transaction model, not the listing model: Vinted (mandatory buyer protection + integrated shipping in one vertical) and Aukro (fee-based marketplace with payments and cross-border expansion). Both now out-visit Sbazar.
- The CZ/SK market has already accepted buyer-paid protection fees (Vinted 18 CZK + 5 %), so a Nordic/OLX-style optional escrow with locker shipping (Packeta/Zásielkovňa, Balíkovo, Alza boxes) is not culturally alien; the risk is the regressive floor fee on low-value items.
- Bank iD exists and is used by Sbazar for a badge; no CZ/SK general portal yet combines Bank iD + escrow + shipping + ratings, which is the Nordic bundle — that is the open slot.
